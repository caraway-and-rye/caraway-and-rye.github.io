# Berbere chicken and egg stew

Serves 4 to 6.

### Ingredients:

- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter or ghee
- 2 large red onions, finely diced
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons minced ginger
- 3 tablespoons berbere spice mix (homemade or store-bought)
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more as needed
- 2 medium vine-ripened tomatoes, finely diced
- 1 cup water
- 8 chicken drumsticks
- 6 large eggs, hard-boiled and peeled

### To serve:

- cooked rice, or a flatbread such as kicha (Eritrean flatbread)

### How to make the stew:

Melt the **butter** in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the **onion, garlic and ginger** and cook, stirring now and then, until they just start to soften, about 5 minutes.

Stir in the **berbere and salt** and keep stirring until the spices smell strongly aromatic, about a minute. Add the **tomatoes** and cook, stirring, until they collapse and the mixture is nearly dry, about 10 minutes.

Pour in the **water**, turn the heat to high and bring to a boil, then lower the heat to a simmer. Season the **chicken** all over with salt and settle the pieces into the pot. Cover and cook, lifting the lid to stir every so often, until the chicken is very tender, about 1 hour.

Add the **eggs** and cook, stirring occasionally, until they are hot through and coated in sauce, about 10 minutes.

Lift the chicken and eggs out with a slotted spoon onto a serving dish. Turn the heat to high, bring the sauce back to a boil and let it reduce and thicken slightly, about 5 minutes. Spoon it over the chicken and eggs and serve straight away.

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for a few days. Reheat gently in a heavy pot over low heat, stirring as it warms.
