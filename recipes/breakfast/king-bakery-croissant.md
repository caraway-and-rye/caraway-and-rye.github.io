## Method

**Not recorded on the card.** Lamination detail — number of folds, resting times, proof temperature — was never written down.

## Notes

- **The columns were unlabelled on the original card.** They are labelled here by the bakery's own convention, where batch size in quarts means quarts of liquid at 2 lb per quart: 16 lb milk = 8 qt. Every other recipe in the collection follows the same rule.
- **Four figures differ from the card, deliberately.** Salt and yeast in the two smallest columns had been rounded up to something weighable in ounces. Scaling exactly from 8 qt drops those roundings.

  | | Column | Card as written | Used here |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Salt & Yeast | 1 qt | 1½ oz → 42.5g | 35.4g |
  | Salt & Yeast | ½ qt | 1 oz → 28.3g | 17.7g |

- **Salt and yeast are the same weight** on the card — 10 oz each at 8 qt. Both land at 1.95% of flour, which is plausible for salt and plausible for *fresh* yeast, but far too high for instant. **The card never says which yeast.** Worth pinning down before you bake: at 1.95% instant, this would blow out.
- **Roll-in butter is 18.75% of flour**, or about 11% of total dough weight. That is lean for a croissant — most formulas run 20–25% of dough weight. Not necessarily wrong, but it makes for a more bread-like, less shatteringly flaky croissant. Recorded as written.
- **Costs on the original card**: $24.51 / $12.25 / $6.13 / $3.06 / $1.53 per batch. Historical, undated — useful only as a ratio.
