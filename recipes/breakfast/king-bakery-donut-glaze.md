# King Bakery Donut Glaze

## Ingredients

| Ingredient      | Amount |
| --------------- | ------ |
| Powdered Sugar  | 2268g  |
| Honey           | 59mL   |
| Cream of Tartar | ½ tsp (~1.5g) |
| Vanilla         | 10mL   |
| Hot Water       | to consistency |

## How to Make

Combine everything, then add hot water a little at a time until you have a smooth glaze that isn't too runny.

**Better to be thick at the start than too thin** — you can always loosen it, but you can't easily take water back out once the sugar has dissolved.

## Notes

- Only one batch size was recorded. It scales linearly if you need less.
- Cream of tartar keeps the glaze from going grainy and helps it set with a slight sheen rather than a chalky crust.
- The honey is doing texture work as much as flavour — it's an invert sugar, so it keeps the glaze from crystallising hard.
- Compare with the [cinnamon bun icing](/recipes/breakfast/king-bakery-cinnamon-bun-icing), which is deliberately much stiffer than this.
