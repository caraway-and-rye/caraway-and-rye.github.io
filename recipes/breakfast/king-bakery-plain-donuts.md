## Notes

- **Two figures differ from the card, deliberately.** Vanilla in the two smallest columns had been rounded to whole spoons. Everything else on this card — every weight in every column — scaled exactly, which is unusually clean for the collection.

  | Column | Card as written | Used here |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Vanilla, ½ qt | 1 tsp → 4.9mL | 7.4mL |
  | Vanilla, ¼ qt | ½ tsp → 2.5mL | 3.7mL |

- **The ¼ qt column already used grams** on the original card — 3.5g for salt, soda and nutmeg — which is exactly ¹⁄₃₂ of 4 oz. Whoever wrote it had already started thinking metric at small scale.
- **Batch size in quarts is literally quarts of buttermilk here** — the 8 qt column takes 8 qt. That is what confirms the convention across the whole collection.
- Eggs at the ¼ qt column were written as a count (2 eggs), consistent with the bakery's 16 large eggs per quart.
- Salt, soda and nutmeg are all the same weight at every size, which makes the mise-en-place easy but also easy to double up by mistake. Weigh them into separate bowls.
- Nutmeg at this level is the defining flavour of an old-style cake donut. Grate it fresh if you can — pre-ground at 113g will taste dusty.
- No frying temperature or time was recorded.
