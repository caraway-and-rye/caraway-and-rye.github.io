# King Bakery Sweet Dough

The base dough for cinnamon buns: roll [Cinnamon Smear](/recipes/breakfast/king-bakery-cinnamon-smear) into it, then finish with [Cinnamon Bun Icing](/recipes/breakfast/king-bakery-cinnamon-bun-icing).

## Ingredients

**The 8 qt column is the source of truth.** Every other column is scaled exactly from it, and every figure on the original card already scaled exactly — nothing was adjusted.

### Flavouring

**4 parts vanilla extract to 1 part lemon extract.** The card gives it by feel rather than by weight — roughly two capfuls vanilla to half a capful lemon.

## Method

**Not recorded on the card.** Mixing time, bulk ferment, and proof were never written down.

## Notes

- Total flour is 36 lb at 8 qt (28 lb bread + 8 lb pastry, a 3.5:1 blend). Against that: milk 44%, eggs 22%, sugar 22%, shortening 22%, salt 1.4%, yeast 2.8%.
- **The card never says which yeast.** At 2.8% of flour this reads as fresh yeast; if it were instant it would be roughly triple a normal dose.
- Batch size in quarts means quarts of liquid at 2 lb per quart — 16 lb milk = 8 qt. The same convention runs through the whole collection.
- Pastry flour softens what would otherwise be a very strong dough. If you substitute, don't just use all bread flour.
